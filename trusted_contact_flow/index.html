1

Notification Settings

Screen · subscriber

The subscriber opens Notification Settings. A Trusted Contacts panel explains the feature and offers one action: Add a Trusted Contact.

Final copy

Notifications include your name and the type of alert, but not the details or your account information – only you can see the full details.

Les notifications indiquent votre nom et le type d’alerte, mais pas les détails ni les renseignements de votre compte – les détails complets ne sont visibles que par vous.

Add a Trusted Contact

Screen · subscriber

The subscriber enters the contact’s first name, last name and email, then picks which alert types the contact receives: Identity, Credit or Scam.

Final copy

Sélectionnez les notifications que cette personne de confiance recevra :

3

Confirm the contact’s details

Screen · subscriber

The subscriber checks the name and email before anything is sent. Cancel returns to the form.

Final copy

Will receive notifications for all selected types of alerts. Please confirm their email address is correct.

Recevra des notifications pour tous les types d’alerte sélectionnés. Veuillez confirmer que son adresse courriel est exacte.

Subscriber clicks Yes, add Trusted Contact

Invitation sent, contact pending

System

Two emails go out. The contact is held as pending, and no alerts reach them until they accept.

Sent to the address the subscriber typed

4

Invitation to accept

Email · to the contact

Says who invited them, what ID Assist is, what they would receive, and which alert types are shared. One button: Accept.

Final copy

Subject: Action Required

Objet : Action requise

By clicking ‘Accept’, you agree to receive email notifications when {{FirstName}} receives the alerts listed above. You can stop receiving them at any time. You also agree to {{ProductName}}’s Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

En cliquant sur « Accepter », vous acceptez de recevoir par courriel des notifications lorsque {{FirstName}} reçoit les alertes énumérées ci-dessus. Vous pouvez cesser de les recevoir en tout temps. Vous acceptez également les Conditions et la Politique de confidentialité applicables à {{ProductName}}.

An alert triggers

System

An Identity, Credit or Scam (High Risk) alert fires on the subscriber’s account. The matching email goes to each accepted contact who was given that alert type, at the same time the subscriber is notified.

Sent when the alert fires

7

Alert email, three versions

Email · to the contact

Names the subscriber and the alert type, describes what was found in general terms, and asks the contact to reach out. No login and no account details.

Final copy

Subject: Credit Alert for {{FirstName}} {{LastName}}

Objet : Alerte de crédit pour {{FirstName}} {{LastName}}

We’ve detected a change in {{FirstName}}’s credit bureau file.

Nous avons détecté un changement au dossier de crédit de {{FirstName}}.

Final copy

Subject: Identity Alert for {{FirstName}} {{LastName}}

Objet : Alerte d’identité pour {{FirstName}} {{LastName}}

We’ve detected a possible online exposure of personal information that {{FirstName}} registered for dark web monitoring.

Nous avons détecté une possible exposition en ligne de renseignements personnels que {{FirstName}} a enregistrés pour la surveillance du Web clandestin.

Final copy

Subject: Scam Alert for {{FirstName}} {{LastName}}

Objet : Alerte d’arnaque pour {{FirstName}} {{LastName}}

Scan & Score has analyzed a communication {{FirstName}} received and identified it as High Risk.

Analyse & Évaluation a analysé une communication reçue par {{FirstName}} et l’a jugée à risque élevé.

Contact reaches out to the subscriber

8

Subscriber reviews the details

Step · subscriber

The contact calls or messages the subscriber, who signs in to see the full alert. The contact never sees the details and can’t sign in.

Final copy (FAQ)

They cannot log in to your account, view your personal information, or make any changes to your settings.

Elle ne peut pas ouvrir une session dans votre compte, consulter vos renseignements personnels ni modifier vos paramètres.

At any time after a contact is added

9

Remove a Trusted Contact

Screen · subscriber

The subscriber removes a contact in Notification Settings. A confirmation asks them to be sure, and alerts to that contact stop. The same screen also lets them change alert types; the deck has no copy or contact notice for that.

Final copy

Are you sure you want to remove {{TrustedContactName}}? They will no longer receive notifications of your alerts.

Voulez-vous vraiment retirer {{TrustedContactName}}? Cette personne ne recevra plus de notifications de vos alertes.

  • Gap

Subscriber confirms the removal

10

Removal notice

Email · to the contact

Tells the contact they were removed, in case it was a mistake that could leave the subscriber exposed. No action needed.

Final copy

Subject: {{FirstName}} {{LastName}} has removed you as a Trusted Contact

Objet : {{FirstName}} {{LastName}} a mis fin à votre rôle de personne de confiance

No action is required on your part. If you believe this was a mistake, please contact {{FirstName}} directly.

Aucune action n’est requise de votre part. Si vous croyez qu’il s’agit d’une erreur, veuillez communiquer directement avec {{FirstName}}.
