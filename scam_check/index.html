How do I get an .eml file?

Gmail (on the web): open the email, click the three dots (⋮) at the top right of the message, then Download message.

Outlook on the web or the new Outlook: open the email, click the three dots (…), then Save as. Wait for the .eml file to download.

Classic Outlook for Windows: dragging an email to your desktop makes a .msg file, which this page can't read. Open the same email in Outlook on the web instead and use Save as.

Saving the email itself keeps its original headers, which is where the sender authentication checks (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) come from. A screenshot or copied text won't work.
