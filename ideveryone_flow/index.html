IDeveryone

Automated steps

Recipient

The person being verified

1

Start a new challenge

Screen · requester

After signing in to the KnowScam portal, the requester opens IDeveryone and clicks Start New Challenge. Recent Activity lists earlier challenges with their status.

  • Attempts 1–3
2

Enter the recipient’s details

Screen · requester

The requester types the recipient’s first name, last name and email address. A checkbox confirms the recipient has agreed to receive messages from Scamnetic, and that those messages may include the requester’s name. Submit for Verification sends the request.

  • Attempts 1–3

Request goes to the recipient

System

IDeveryone sends a KnowScam email to the address entered. The link in it expires at a stated date and time.

3

Verification email arrives

Email · recipient

The email says the requester (here, Geoff Thomas) has asked the recipient to verify their identity. A Complete Verification button opens the process. The email gives an expiry date and a help address.

  • Attempts 1–3
4

Choose a language

Screen · recipient

The recipient lands on an IDeveryone page that names the requester. They pick English, Français or Español and click Continue. The page says no personal information is shared with anyone, including the requester.

  • Attempts 1–3
5

Agree to share location

Screen · recipient

A one-time location request. The page says location is not tracked continuously or stored for later. The recipient chooses I Do Not Accept or Share My Location.

  • Attempts 1–3
6

Accept Terms and Biometric agreement

Screen · recipient

Two documents can be opened (Terms of Service, Biometric Data Use Agreement) and two boxes ticked. The second box covers personal data, the Scamnetic Privacy Notice, an Australian notice and the Biometric Data Use Agreement. I Accept is greyed out until both are ticked.

  • Attempts 1–3
7

Verify current location

Screen · recipient

The recipient enters a postal code and country. The page says the location is used only for verification and is not shared with the requester.

  • Attempts 1–3

Which check comes next?

System

The recipient’s next screen differed between attempts. In attempt 1 they were asked for a phone number and a code. In attempts 2 and 3 they went straight to a selfie.

Result reaches the requester

11

Portal: unable to verify

Screen · requester

Recent Activity shows Fail. The result panel lists the date, the name the recipient gave, the verified email and location, and a phone check marked as failed. Next steps advise reporting suspicious activity, being wary of a retry with different contact details, and caution with meetings, information or money.

Attempts 2 and 3: IDeveryone asks for a selfie check

12

Verify with a selfie

Screen · recipient

After the same steps 1–7, the recipient goes to a selfie page. It says the image is used only for verification and won’t be shared with the requester. Begin Verification starts the check.

  • Attempts 2 and 3
13

Continue on your phone

Screen · recipient

A new window explains the handoff: scan a QR code with the phone camera, finish the steps on the phone, then return to the desktop. It has a different look from the earlier IDeveryone pages and offers its own language menu.

  • Attempts 2 and 3
14

Scan the QR code

Screen · recipient

A unique QR code is shown. The recipient scans it, opens the link on their phone and takes the selfie there. The phone-side screens weren’t captured.

  • Attempts 2 and 3
15

Wait on the desktop

Screen · recipient

While the selfie is taken on the phone, the desktop says not to close the page. It continues once the phone steps are done.

  • Attempts 2 and 3
16

All finished

Screen · recipient

The same page as step 10, shown after a failed attempt (2) and a passed one (3). The recipient can’t tell the outcome from it.

  • Attempts 2 and 3

Selfie result goes to the requester

System

IDeveryone emails the requester and updates the portal. Attempt 2 produced two emails, sent three minutes apart. Attempt 3 is shown in the portal.

Attempt 2: first result

17

Email: verified

Email · requester

The first email says the recipient is verified and that the requester can proceed with confidence, and tells them to log in to the mobile app for results. In the portal the contact showed as verified. Sent 16:44.

  • Attempt 2
  • Says verified
18

Email: could not complete

Email · requester

Three minutes later (16:47) a second email with the same Scan ID says the evaluation couldn’t be completed. It lists three possible causes: details not verified, the recipient declined, or they took too long. It adds next steps, including offering a second try by text message.

  • Attempt 2
  • Says not verified
19

Portal: failed biometric

Screen · requester

Back in the portal, the contact now shows Fail. The panel lists Failed biometric verification under Biometric, with the Scan ID from the emails.

  • Attempt 2
  • Fail

Attempt 3: the selfie check passes

20

Portal: passed

Screen · requester

On the third attempt the selfie check passed. The panel is marked Scamnetic Reviewed and lists the email checks (name match with historical records, no link to known malicious activity, no evidence of identity compromise) plus Passed biometric verification. Next steps remind the requester that even verified people may not act in their best interests.

  • Attempt 3
  • Pass
